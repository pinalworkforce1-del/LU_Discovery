NOVA'S RÉSUMÉ REVIEW
Email check

Nova will flag an obviously informal résumé email and explain why a simple professional address may help.

Date relevance check

Nova can flag older dates or experience that may not strengthen this targeted résumé, without inferring age.

Target-job gap check

When a job description is provided, Nova separates supported qualifications from gaps you should not claim.

Nova's honesty rule

A strong résumé makes the truth easier for an employer to see. It never adds experience, credentials, numbers, duties, or accomplishments you can't support.
